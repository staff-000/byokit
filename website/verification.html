Every speed measurement helps.

Models supported by BYOKit’s text-generation adapters appear by default. Uncheck “Callable only” to browse all catalog models. Call support does not guarantee access for your account. Availability and speed are separate. Standard speed summaries use the median and middle 50% of published comparable measurements from the last 30 days, grouped by provider offering and benchmark version. Browser location, network, provider load, and output length still affect results. Batch checks verify availability; use Test speed for the two-call speed benchmark.

Attempts are saved here with their outcome, model/API settings, date, latency, and available token usage. Keys, prompts, and generated text are never saved.
